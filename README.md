# Analysing Cost of Living and Inflation Using ONS Data

## Project Overview

The UK Office for National Statistics (ONS) publishes regularly updated datasets on prices, household expenditure, and inflation. In this project, we have narrowed our focus to explore a specific macroeconomic question: **How do historical wars and conflicts affect inflation rates in the UK?** Our analysis combines data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and visualisation, alongside statistical methods to model trends and measure the economic impact of global conflicts on the UK's cost of living.

**Data Source:** [Office for National Statistics (ONS) - Inflation and price indices](https://www.ons.gov.uk/economy/inflationandpriceindices)
